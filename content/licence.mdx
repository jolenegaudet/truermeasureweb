This is the agreement covering **how you may use Truer Measure™ and what happens to your child's records**. The plans, payment, renewal and cancellation are covered separately in our **Terms of Service**. What we collect and why is in our **Privacy Policy**.

## 1. Who this is between

This agreement is between you, the parent or legal guardian who holds the account, and **VisionEd Consultation Inc., operating as Truer Measure™**, a company based in New Brunswick, Canada.

Your child is not a party to it. Neither is your child's school, school board, or any professional whose report you upload.

## 2. Your authority to be here

By creating a record for a child, **you confirm that you are that child's parent or legal guardian, and that you have the authority to share their educational and developmental records with us and to receive our analysis of them.**

We rely on that confirmation. We do not verify custody arrangements, contact other guardians, or interpret court orders.

**If someone else with legal responsibility for your child raises a competing claim with us, we will pause: we will stop new readings, and freeze the record, until you resolve it between you or send us a court order or signed agreement.** We will tell you if this happens. We will not take a side in a family dispute.

## 3. What we are, and what we are not

**We are:** an interpretive service. The app reads the documents and notes you give it, pulls out what each source actually said, looks for patterns that repeat across years and observers, and offers an interpretation of what those patterns may suggest. **Every reading is written by the app's software, with the help of an AI model. No person writes it.**

**We are not, and you must not rely on us as:**

- a psychological, psychoeducational or cognitive **assessment**
- a **label** for your child, or a screening for any condition
- **medical, psychological or therapeutic advice**
- a basis for a **special education, placement, streaming or accommodation** decision
- a **forecast** of your child's future
- a substitute for **professional judgement**
- your child's official **school record**: this is our analysis, not their file

Where a reading quotes a psychologist, physician or therapist, we are repeating what that person wrote and attributing it to them. We are not adopting it, extending it, or forming a medical or psychological view of our own.

**Every decision about your child remains yours.** Do not delay or avoid getting professional advice because of something we wrote. If you believe your child is at risk or in crisis, contact a qualified professional or emergency services, not us.

## 4. Reliance and accuracy

We commit to showing our reasoning. **Interpretations that a reading presents as grounded in your child's documents trace back to specific, attributed records, and you can inspect that chain.** Reflective passages, general research references and suggested strategies are not evidence-grounded in that sense, and readings distinguish them.

Where source material is unreadable or ambiguous, we mark it uncertain and leave it out of the reasoning rather than guessing what it probably said.

**That is a promise about traceability, not about correctness.** Interpreting a child's development is genuinely uncertain. Faithfully-grounded reasoning can still reach the wrong conclusion, and thoughtful people, including the teachers who wrote your documents, may read the same evidence differently. We commit that our reasoning is honest and visible. We cannot commit that our conclusions are right.

**You can correct the evidence; the findings are ours.** If something was read wrongly from a document, you can correct it yourself in the app, and you can delete any record. The readings are then written again from the corrected record. We do not otherwise change a reading because you disagree with it.

## 5. Your records stay yours

**You keep ownership of everything you upload and everything you write.** Nothing here transfers it to us.

You give us permission to store, back up, process, analyse and write readings from your material, **only** to run the service for your family, to keep the record accumulating so later readings can build on earlier ones, to meet our security and legal obligations, and for internal quality review.

### What we will not do

We will not publish your material, sell or license it, use it in marketing, use it to serve advertising, use it for another family, or share it outside those purposes.

**We will not use your documents, your observations, your child's record, or your readings to train or improve any artificial intelligence model, ours or anyone else's.** Our AI service provider states that it does not use what its commercial customers send through its API to train its models without their express permission, and we have not given it. We will not change this for records you have already given us without asking you first and getting a yes.

### The readings themselves

**Your child's readings are yours.** You may keep them, print them, and share them with anyone involved in your child's life: the other parent, family, your child, teachers, tutors, professionals. That right is permanent and survives the end of your subscription.

Please don't publish them publicly or use them commercially. We keep the rights in our own methodology, framework, templates and vocabularies: the machinery, not your child's story.

## 6. Processing by artificial intelligence

The app writes your child's readings with the help of an AI language model from our AI service provider, in the United States, used through its API, which helps pull out what each record says, find patterns and write the reading. You agree to that on the agreement screen, before you first add anything.

**Before text is sent to the model, we replace the names the record knows** (your child's, yours, your child's teachers' and schools', and the people you name as a source) **with stand-ins, and remove email addresses, phone numbers, postal codes, street addresses, links and long numbers.** The stand-ins are put back in the answer inside our own systems. **A document's pages, and photos, are sent as they are**, because the model has to see them to read them, so whatever is printed on them goes with them. A name the record does not know is not replaced. **This reduces how easily your family can be identified; it does not prevent it, and it is not anonymisation.**

**What an AI-written reading can get wrong.** A language model can misread a document, over-weight a single comment, or state something with more confidence than the evidence allows. That is why every interpretation points back to the records it rests on (section 4), and why a reading is not an assessment and not professional advice, and does not label your child or forecast their future (section 3). If something looks wrong, tell us.

Your records are stored in Canada. Content sent to the AI model is stored and processed by our AI service provider in the United States only (Privacy Policy section 6).

Truer Measure™'s team will access your child's record for quality and operational delivery purposes as needed.

- **Everything:** every child and every file. Your account stays.
- **Your account:** the account and everything in it. You cannot close it while a subscription is still set to renew: cancel first, so a closed account can never go on being charged.

**You can also ask us:** email privacy@truermeasure.com. We delete it from active systems within 30 days and from backups within their normal expiry cycle. What was already sent to our AI service provider is kept for the period it sets (Privacy Policy section 6). Backups keep deleted material until they expire.
