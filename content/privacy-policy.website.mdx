## 2. The short version

- On this website we collect what you type into a form, and nothing else about you.
- **Nothing is stored on your device.** No cookie, no identifier, no tracker.
- Our host works out which country your visit comes from, so a price can be shown in your
  currency. It is used for that and not kept.
- **We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it for advertising. We never have and we
  will not.**
- **We do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.**
- You can see it, correct it, or have it deleted.

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 The three forms on this website

There are three, and they are not the same.

| Form | You must give | You may also give | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Waitlist and enquiry** | first name, last name, email address | phone number; number of children and their ages; city; province or state; postal code | GoHighLevel |
| **"Be the first to know" email list** | email address | nothing: we ask for nothing else | Kit |
| **Right of withdrawal** | first name, last name, email address | nothing | GoHighLevel |

The first column is what the form will not submit without. Everything in the second column can be
left blank, and the form works.

We use what you give us only to contact you about Truer Measure. **We do not sell it and we do not
share it for advertising.** Ask us at privacy@truermeasure.com and we will delete it.

**Please don't include your children's names in a form.** The children field is a free-text box, so
nothing stops you, which is exactly why we are asking. How many children and what ages is all we
request, and all we need.

### 3.2 What the email list keeps

If you join the "be the first to know" list, Kit holds **your email address**, and these three
things about the signup itself:

- **when** you signed up, to the minute
- **which page** you signed up from
- **which language** you were reading in

We record them once, at your first signup, so that we can show how and when you agreed to hear
from us. If you ever sign up again from somewhere else, the original record is what we keep. We do
not ask for your name, your phone number, or anything about your child.

We use the list to send you launch news and occasional product updates. **Every one of those
emails carries an unsubscribe link.** You can also ask us at privacy@truermeasure.com and we will
remove you.

### 3.3 What your visit alone tells us

**We count visits.** We use Plausible Analytics to see how many people come and which pages they
read. It sets no cookie and collects no personal information. We see totals, not people. Section 5
says more.

**We work out your country, for the price.** Pages that show a price show it in Canadian dollars,
US dollars or euros. Our host, Netlify, resolves the country a request comes from and tells the
page, which picks one of the three. That country is used to choose a currency and for nothing
else: it is not stored, not added to any record about you, and not sent to anyone.

**When a form fails, we record the failure and not your details.** If a submission cannot be
saved, our server records the error code and a one-word reason so that we can fix it. What you
typed is not written to any log.

Our host keeps its own technical records of requests to this website, as every host does. We do
not use them to identify visitors and we do not combine them with anything in section 3.

## 5. Cookies, measurement, and your country

**Nothing is stored on your device by this website.** No cookie is set, no identifier is written,
nothing is kept in your browser's storage. **We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or
cross-site trackers**, and no part of this site is embedded from somewhere else, so no other
company sees your visit unless you click a link to it.

**We count visits with Plausible Analytics.** Plausible is **cookieless**: it sets no cookie,
stores no identifier on your device, does not follow you across websites, and does not collect
personal information. We see which pages are read and how many people read them. It is the only
service on this site that your browser contacts, and the fonts and everything else are served from
truermeasure.com.

**Your country, once, for the price.** Netlify resolves which country a request comes from, which
it does from the connection itself, and the page uses it to show one of three currencies. We ask
nothing of you, no precise location is involved, and nothing about it is stored, recorded against
you, or passed on.

**That is why you are not being shown a cookie consent banner — there is nothing here to consent
to.** If we ever adopt anything that does track you, we will ask first.

## 6. Your rights

You can, at any time, by writing to privacy@truermeasure.com:

- **See** what we hold about you
- **Correct** anything wrong
- **Delete** what we hold about you
- **Get a copy** in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent**, which for the email list also means the unsubscribe link in any email

These are your rights, not favours we are granting.

**The legal deadline, and what we promise on top of it.** Where the privacy law that applies to us
sets a time limit for answering a request like the ones above, that limit applies and we meet it.
Separately, and whichever limit applies, **we commit to answering you within 30 days, and there is
no charge.** The 30 days are our own undertaking; they do not replace a shorter deadline if one
applies to you.

**You can also complain to us**, at the same address, and get a real answer. A complaint, or a
general question about this policy, is not a request to exercise one of the rights above. We do not
put a fixed deadline on those; we answer as soon as we can.

**If our answer doesn't satisfy you**, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner
of Canada (priv.gc.ca), or — if you live in Quebec — to the Commission d'accès à l'information
(cai.gouv.qc.ca).
