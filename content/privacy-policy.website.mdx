## 2. The short version

- On this website we collect what you type into a form, and nothing else about you.
- **We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for advertising. We never have and we will not.**
- **We do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.**
- You can see it, correct it, or have it deleted.

---

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 If you fill in a form on our website

Our waitlist and enquiry forms ask for your **name, email address, phone number, city, province or
state, postal code, and optionally how many children you have and their ages**. We use this only to
contact you about Truer Measure.

**This information is stored with GoHighLevel, a service provider in the United States** — see
section 4. We do not sell it and we do not share it for advertising. Ask us at privacy@truermeasure.com
and we will delete it.

**Please don't include your children's names in a form.** Ages are all we ask for, and all we need.

### 3.2 If you join the "be the first to know" email list

On our website you can leave **your email address** to hear when the next version of Truer Measure
is ready. That is the only thing we ask for, and the only thing we store. We do not ask for your
name, your phone number, or anything about your child.

We use it to send you launch news and occasional product updates. **Every one of those emails
carries an unsubscribe link.** You can also ask us at privacy@truermeasure.com and we will remove you.

**This list is held with Kit, an email service provider in the United States** (see section 4). We
record the date you signed up and which page you signed up from, so that we can show how and when
you agreed to hear from us. We do not sell this list and we do not share it for advertising.

## 4. Who else touches your information

We use service providers to run this website. They act on our instructions, are bound by contract,
and may not use your information for their own purposes.

| Provider | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **GoHighLevel** | Waitlist and enquiry forms | United States |

**Otherwise, we disclose your information only:** when you tell us to; when the law requires it or
a valid legal order compels it; or, if the business is ever sold or merged, to the buyer — **and
then only if the buyer takes on the commitments in this policy in full.** We will tell you before
that happens.

**We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.**

## 5. Cookies and website analytics

**We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site trackers.**

**On our website we use Plausible Analytics** to count visits and see which pages people read.
Plausible is **cookieless**: it sets no cookie, stores no identifier on your device, does not
follow you across websites, and does not collect personal information. We see totals, not people.

**That is why you are not being shown a cookie consent banner — there is nothing here to consent
to.** If we ever adopt anything that does track you, we will ask first.

---

## 6. Your rights

You can, at any time, by writing to privacy@truermeasure.com:

- **See** what we hold about you
- **Correct** anything wrong
- **Delete** what we hold about you
- **Get a copy** in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent**, which for the email list also means the unsubscribe link in any email

**When you make one of the requests above, we respond within 30 days, and there is no charge.**

**You can also complain to us**, at the same address, and get a real answer. A complaint, or a
general question about this policy, is not a request to exercise one of the rights above. We do
not put a fixed deadline on those; we answer as soon as we can.

**If our answer doesn't satisfy you**, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner
of Canada (priv.gc.ca), or — if you live in Quebec — to the Commission d'accès à l'information
(cai.gouv.qc.ca).

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy — particularly what we collect, why, who we share with, or
the commitment not to train models on your information — **we will email you at least 30 days
before it takes effect** and say plainly what changed.

**We will never apply a change backwards to weaken protections that applied to information you
already gave us.**
