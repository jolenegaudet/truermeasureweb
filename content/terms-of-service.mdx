## 1. What you are buying

Truer Measure™ has two plans. **School** is free. **School & Life** is a paid subscription.

**School (free, for three months).** School is free for **three months from the day you add a child, once for each child**. After those three months, that child's record stays in your account for you to read and export, and nothing new can be added until you start School & Life for them. On School, you add your child's report cards, as PDFs or photos. **On School, uploads are report cards only**: a file that does not look like a report card is not added. Each report card is read and explained in plain language, with questions to ask the teacher and ideas for home. You also get a reading across the report cards, and a **first look**: written once, from the report cards and the notes you add when you start (including your notes about life beyond school), and kept. Those notes are the only thing other than report cards that is read on School, and they are read once, to write the first look.

**School & Life (paid).** Everything in School, plus:

- moments from life beyond school: a sentence, photos of what they did or made (not photos that show your child), documents
- other school documents
- readings that bring school and life together
- **Reflect together**: questions you answer with your child, kept in their own words
- suggestions to try together, and plans
- **Ask**: questions about your child's record
- reminder emails, if you turn them on

**Every reading is written by the app's software, with the help of an AI language model, from the record you keep.** No person writes them. The Privacy Policy explains what is sent to the model.

**One account, several children. One subscription, one child.** An account belongs to one parent and can hold several children. **Each child can use School free for three months. A School & Life subscription covers one child, named before you pay.** You can have School & Life for one child while another stays on School. Adding a child never charges you anything: School & Life for another child is a separate purchase, at the same price. There is no discount for brothers and sisters.

**New readings may pause.** We may pause new readings for every family for a short while, to keep the service running; nothing is lost, and readings resume on their own.

The app has a **Feedback** button and a **Suggest a feature** button on every page, for every family. Using Truer Measure™ is **not individual consulting**, and it is not a promise that any suggestion will be built. See section 11.
