## 4. Why we ask where your child is from

*This section exists because the profile screen deliberately doesn't explain it. You are entitled
to the reason, and here it is.*

**The short answer: so the report is about your child, and not a generic one.**

A report card is a set of marks in a context, and the context changes what the marks mean. A
child who arrived in the country two years ago, a child schooled at home, and a child learning in
their third language are not producing the same evidence as each other — even when the marks on
the page are identical. Without knowing that, a system reads every child as the same child, and
the interpretation it produces is worse for exactly the children whose stories are least ordinary.

**Country of origin, years in the country, languages spoken, and school type are context. They
change how the evidence is read. They are never treated as a strength or a deficit in their own
right.**

**What we do with these answers:** we use them to interpret your child's documents more
accurately. That is the entire purpose.

**What we do not do with them, ever:**

- We do not use them to assess, infer, or record immigration or citizenship status.
- We do not share them with any school, government body, or immigration authority.
- We do not use them to decide whether to serve your family, or what to charge you.
- We do not use them to compare your child against other children.
- We do not use them for advertising, marketing segmentation, or any commercial purpose.
- We do not sell them.

**Years in the country is asked only when the country of origin you gave differs from where your
child lives now.** If it is never asked, we hold nothing — a blank means the question never came
up, not that you declined.

**If you would rather not answer these, write to privacy@truermeasure.com before completing the

## 6. Artificial intelligence

We use AI, including third-party large language models, to extract evidence from your documents
and draft analysis.

**Before anything is sent to an outside model, we replace your family's names — your child's,
yours, siblings', teachers', and named professionals' — with placeholders. The map back to real
names never leaves our database.** On the way back, real names are restored inside our own
systems, so you always read real names.

**We are straight with you about the limit.** This reduces identifiability. It does not eliminate
it, because a document can identify a family through context — an unusual school, a specific
programme, a distinctive circumstance — rather than through a name. We commit to the control. We
will not overstate what it achieves.

**Our AI providers are contractually prohibited from using anything we send them to train their
models.** We do not use your information to train, fine-tune or improve any model either. We will
not change this for information you have already given us without asking you and getting a yes.

**No decision about your child is made by a machine alone.** The service produces interpretation,
not decisions. Nothing it outputs determines any entitlement, placement or opportunity, and every
family's first report is reviewed by a person before release.

## 8. Where your information lives

**Your child's records are stored in Canada**, on Microsoft Azure's Canadian region.

Some processing happens outside Canada — anonymised content sent to our AI provider, payment
processing, email delivery and error monitoring. **Information processed outside Canada is subject
to the laws of that country, and may be accessible to its courts and authorities.** We use
providers who commit contractually to protecting it, but we cannot promise a foreign government
will never compel access. You are entitled to know that, so we are telling you.

## 10. Your rights

You can, at any time, by writing to privacy@truermeasure.com:

- **See** what we hold about you and your child
- **Correct** anything wrong
- **Delete** a document, or your child's entire record
- **Get a copy** in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** — though this may mean we can no longer provide the service
- **Ask how** an interpretation was reached, and see the evidence behind it
- **Complain**, and get a real answer

**We respond within 30 days.** There is no charge.

**If our answer doesn't satisfy you**, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner
of Canada (priv.gc.ca), or — if you live in Quebec — to the Commission d'accès à l'information
(cai.gouv.qc.ca).

## 11. Your child's information, and your child's own rights

The service is for parents and guardians. Children do not have accounts and cannot transact with
us. Everything we hold about your child is here because you provided it, on the basis that you
have the authority to do so.

**As your child gets older, the law may give them rights over their own information** — to see it,
or to object to its being held. If a child who is the subject of a record asks us directly, and we
reasonably conclude they have the capacity to make that request, **we may be required to answer
them rather than you, and we may do so.** We will tell you if that happens, unless we are
prohibited from telling you or it would put the child at risk.

**When your child turns 18**, they will be offered an account of their own, so that the person the
record is about can see it.

Where United States law applies, your acceptance of our agreements at checkout is the verifiable
parental consent required for collecting information about a child under 13. You can withdraw it
at any time by asking us to delete the record.

## 12. Cookies and website analytics

**We use one kind of cookie: the one that keeps you signed in.** It is essential to the service
working, and it cannot be turned off without signing you out.

**We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site trackers.**

**On our website we use Plausible Analytics** to count visits and see which pages people read.
Plausible is **cookieless**: it sets no cookie, stores no identifier on your device, does not
follow you across websites, and does not collect personal information. We see totals, not people.

**That is why you are not being shown a cookie consent banner — there is nothing here to consent
to.** If we ever adopt anything that does track you, we will ask first.

We use **passwordless sign-in** — a link sent to your email. **There are no passwords anywhere on
the platform, for anyone.** We cannot lose yours, because we never have one.

## 13. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest · records scoped to your family and enforced at the database
level · access limited to personnel who need it and logged · backed-up, durable storage · secrets
held outside the codebase.

**No system is perfectly secure, and we are not going to tell you ours is.**

**If a breach occurs that creates a real risk of significant harm to you or your child, we will
tell you and notify the regulators, as the law requires** — and we will tell you what happened
rather than a sanitised version of it.
