## 6. Artificial intelligence

**How readings are written.** The app writes every reading with the help of an AI language model from our AI service provider, in the United States, used through its API. No person writes the readings.

**Where it is processed.** Our AI service provider stores what we send it in the United States, and does its preparatory processing there, such as converting images. We have set our account so that the model itself also runs only in the United States.

**What is sent.** For each piece of work, only what that work needs: your child's records, the report card fields, the words read from your documents, and the task's instructions. **On School & Life, your own words about your child are sent too.** On School they are not, except the notes you add when you start, which are read once to write the first look.

**Before text is sent, we replace the names the record knows**: your child's, yours, your child's teachers' and schools', and the people you name as a source. **They become stand-ins, and are put back in the answer inside our own systems**, so you always read real names. **Email addresses, phone numbers, postal codes, street addresses, links and long numbers are removed.**

**What is not replaced.** When a document is read, **its pages are sent as images, as they are**, because the model has to see them to read them: whatever is printed on them goes with them, including names, a school's name and logo, and anything else that identifies your child. A photo is sent as it is. A name the record does not know, such as a friend or a coach you mention in a moment, is not replaced.

**We are straight with you about the limit.** This reduces how easily your family can be identified. It does not prevent it, because a document's pages go as they are, and because a family can be identified through context (an unusual school, a specific programme, a distinctive circumstance) rather than a name. **This is not anonymisation, and we do not claim it is.** We commit to the control. We will not overstate what it achieves.

**Training.** We do not use your information to train, fine-tune or improve any AI model. Our AI service provider states that it does not use what its commercial customers send through its API to train its models without their express permission, and we have not given it.

**How long our AI service provider keeps it.** Our AI service provider states that it deletes what is sent through its API, and what it returns, within 30 days. It may keep them longer where it must to enforce its usage policy (up to two years for material its safety systems flag) or to comply with the law. Deleting a record in the app does not reach what our AI service provider has already received.

**What a reading is, and is not.** A reading is an interpretation of the records you give us. It can be wrong. **It is not an assessment and not professional advice; it does not label your child or forecast their future**, and it does not replace a teacher's, doctor's or psychologist's judgement. Section 3 of the Licence Agreement says more.

**Decisions.** No decision about you or your child is made by the service. Readings inform your decisions; they do not make them.
