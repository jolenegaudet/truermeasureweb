## 1. Who it's for, what it promises

**Hero:** a K–12 parent who notices things about their child and has nowhere to put them.
New Brunswick and South Florida first.

**The promise (founder's words):** *I help parents do something with what they already see in
their child — preserve it, bring it into conversations with school, and make sure grades aren't
the only evidence they have to speak for their child.*

**One-liner:** *Grades shouldn't be the only evidence that speaks for your child. The Hidden
Report Card brings together what school records and what you see — kept across the years,
parent-owned — so you have a fuller account of your child when it counts.*

**Guide:** teacher, principal, education innovator. Her thinking is in the software; she is not
the product. Access to her is a separate offer (Learn From the Room, Inner Circle), explained
on the site, not in posts.

**Weekly spine:** Mon card · Tue reel · Wed card · **Thu product or proof** · Fri question + keyword · weekend Stories.
One strong piece a day, not three. Twenty minutes a day on every comment and DM. Batch-make on one afternoon.

## 5. The path from post to sale

Nobody buys US$597 from a caption. A post produces one of two handoffs:

1. **Post → "comment BEYOND"** → the one-page guide (pack 02) by DM → one follow-up at 48h → the site, or fifteen minutes with the founder and the child's last report card.
2. **Post → link in bio** → homepage → Stripe.

The URL appears in pins and bios only. Everywhere else, the keyword. Exact DM copy: pack 01 §4.

## 6. What to measure, weekly

Guide requests · DMs that mention a child · conversations held · sales attributed "social" (S03) · link taps (18/mo today) · non-follower share of views (FB 46%, IG 38%).
The number the channel is judged on is **guide requests**. Target for ten families from social by January: ~80 requests a month.

## 8. Rules that don't move

- The parent is the subject of the first sentence. The founder lands on the parent within two.
- The founder's own sentences beat written ones — reuse them.
- "Evidence" only ever paired with "beyond grades." Parents' words otherwise: strengths, patterns, moments, who he is.
- Nothing that names a child. Nothing that diagnoses, ranks, or predicts. Nothing in French that isn't true yet.
- No URL outside pins and bios. No tiers in the feed. No nudges to existing families.
