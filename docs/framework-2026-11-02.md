## 3. The French, and the four choices worth her eye

The demo has no French, so these are equivalents, not quotations. They follow
the conventions already on /fr and use the terms a Quebec school uses. Four are
judgment calls rather than obvious translations, and each is one word to change
if she prefers another:

- **Thinking → Réflexion.** "Pensée" is the faculty; "réflexion" is the act,
  which is what the demo's line describes ("making sense of information and
  connecting ideas").
- **Learning from feedback → Apprendre de la rétroaction.** "Rétroaction" is
  the standard term in Quebec schools. "Commentaires reçus" would be plainer
  and longer.
- **Empathy & perspective-taking → Empathie et point de vue de l'autre.**
  "Prise de perspective" is a direct calque and reads as jargon.
- **Cultural understanding → Compréhension interculturelle.** The demo changed
  this from awareness to understanding; "ouverture aux cultures", which the old
  website list used, is openness, which is a different claim.

Two more, for the record: **Independence → Autonomie** (the usual word for a
child doing things without help), and **Integrity → Intégrité**, where the
demo's own id is `honesty` but the name a parent reads is Integrity, so the
French follows the name.

### Taken off, because the framework does not have them

**Imagination**, **Resilience**, **Emotional Intelligence**, **Civic-Mindedness**.

Emotional intelligence is the one to know about: the demo deliberately does not
make it an entry. It describes it as what the *Themselves and others* family
shows between them, in the demo's own words: "knowing themselves, understanding
and managing feelings, seeing someone else's side and getting along with
others." So it is still on the page, as four entries rather than one label.

### Added, because the framework has them and the website did not

All six foundations (**Reading**, **Writing**, **Communicating**,
**Mathematics**, **Thinking**, **Problem solving**), plus **Learning from
feedback** and **Movement & physical skills**.

### And the grouping, which the website did not have at all

The old list was eight rows of four words with hairline rules between them,
which read as categories without being any. The page now carries the demo's own
two groups and four family headings.

## 5. The section heading, settled

The page used to open this section with **"None of these receive a grade. Yet
they shape so much of who your child is capable of becoming."** That line is
true of the twenty-one and false of the six, since school grades reading,
writing and mathematics for years, so it was moved down to introduce the
characteristics alone.

She replaced it the same day, judging it "too absolute, even when restricted to
the 21 characteristics", and gave the wording herself:

> **Your child's grades don't show everything they're capable of.**
>
> « Les notes de votre enfant ne montrent pas tout ce dont il est capable. »

That is true of both groups, so the reason for moving it is gone and the
heading is back above the whole section, where it was. The foundations keep the
demo's own two sentences under their own heading. Nothing was written for the
website.
