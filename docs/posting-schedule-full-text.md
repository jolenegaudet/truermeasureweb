# Posting schedule — the series first, then one pain a week

*7 September – 23 October 2026. Days one to six are the founder's series, *There's More to Your Child Than School Can Measure*, one part a day, exactly as written. Then five weeks, each owned by one pain parents described in their own words (r/Parenting, r/ADHDparenting, Mumsnet — quoted as written, trimmed, no usernames, no children's names), and a closing week, *Keep looking*. Read top to bottom; every post for every platform is written out in full.*

*Instagram: three a day. Facebook: one a day. LinkedIn: every series day, then Monday / Wednesday / Friday. Substack: every series day, then nothing until the series is collected. Weekends after the series: Stories only, or one personal post.*

*A pain week's shape: **Monday** the pain, in their words (the HEIT reel and two quote cards) · **Tuesday** the tool (a reel and a carousel a parent can use tonight) · **Wednesday** the Read — three real report-card lines read on camera, each with what it can hold, what it can't, and the question to ask · **Thursday** the product, for that week's parent · **Friday** the week's question, and a quote card that hands off to next week.*

**The only call to action, everywhere:** DM "BEYOND" to start.
